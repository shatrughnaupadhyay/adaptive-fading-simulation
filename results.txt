TABLE IX: Calibration -- Power-Law Learning Curve (k_t = a * t^b)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Fitted: k_t = 0.4087 * t^0.0380    R^2 = 0.8444
  Calibration target (Type-B): power-law SHAPE (monotone, concave,
    single-exponent goodness-of-fit) per Newell & Rosenbloom (1981).
  Note: their psychomotor range b in [0.2, 0.5] is NOT the target
    here. Continuous bounded mastery, archetype pooling (incl. Experts
    near cap), and the 80-episode window structurally suppress the
    exponent magnitude by ~one order. Shape, not magnitude, is the fit.
